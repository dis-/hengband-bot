PLAN-STEP: 3 / ISSUE: #46 — Light swap diagnosis

Evidence
========
The 23:12 autorecover decision capture contains 90 rows: 43 `restore-lantern`
and 42 `equipment-transaction:equip` decisions. The sampled fixed-state boards
alternate between the same two arrangements. At turn 514401 the equipped
light is a Brass Lantern (`tval=39, sval=1, light_turns=0`), with a Wooden
Torch (`tval=39, sval=0, light_turns=1866`) in inventory and five oil flasks
(`tval=77`). At turn 514408 the torch is equipped and the empty lantern is in
the pack. The sequence repeats through turn 514948. Player light radius
alternates 2/1 accordingly. The decisions carry the same `wl` key, confirming
that both decisions address the light slot.

(a) Why a zero-turn lantern is restored
---------------------------------------
At `src/hengbot/policy.py:11464-11469`, before normal light selection,
`_decide` calls `_empty_lantern_to_restore` and immediately returns an
`_equipment_wield` command when it gets an item. The predicate at
`src/hengbot/policy.py:12831-12864` requires no usable permanent light, at
least one oil flask, and no currently equipped permanent light or lantern
above `LANTERN_REFILL_FUEL`. It then explicitly returns an identified, not
cursed/broken lantern with `fuel <= LANTERN_REFILL_FUEL`; zero qualifies. On
the torch-equipped capture board, those conditions hold: no permanent light,
oil in the pack, torch currently equipped, empty lantern carried. The helper
does not require the lantern itself to be usable before it wields it; its
purpose is to get it into the slot for a subsequent refill.

This restoration behavior was introduced by commit `d6cd3975` (2026-07-24).
The helper is reasonable in isolation; its interaction with the separately
selecting equipment optimizer was never handled. The repeat alternation is a
regression of having these two owners run sequentially without a common
selection rule, rather than a failure to recognize that zero fuel is empty.

(b) Why the equipment transaction equips the torch
-------------------------------------------------
`_equipment_wield` routes the chosen slot change into the equipment transaction
owner. The capture's transaction execution at decision sequence 11139 names
`phase='equip'`, `item_id='pack:441248f0e6daf1d7:0'`, `target_slot='light'`; the
board at turn 514437 shows the torch in that slot and the lantern in inventory.
The optimizer predicate `operational_equipment_candidate` at
`src/hengbot/equipment_optimizer.py:251-261` excludes a known carried/equipped
consumable light when `fuel <= 0`. `usable_light_candidate` at lines 869-875
uses that predicate, so the empty lantern never enters loadout search. The
usable, fueled torch does.

The score at `src/hengbot/equipment_optimizer.py:299-308` ranks permanent
lights 2, lanterns 1, and torches 0; `_better_loadout` compares that rank only
when combat metrics are identical (`:1008-1015`). Thus a usable lantern beats
a torch on a complete combat tie; otherwise combat score can take precedence.
In this capture the optimizer is not comparing the empty lantern to the torch
at all. Its chosen torch reflects that omission plus its permitted loadout,
not a higher torch light score. This optimizer behavior is a pre-existing
selection policy (light quality ranking was added in `56ac155d`); the case of
an empty-but-refillable lantern versus torch was never represented.

(c) Why the two owners never converge
-------------------------------------
The survival restore selector sees that the lantern is empty, oil exists, and
the equipped torch is not a lantern or permanent light; it does not ask whether
the optimizer can retain that lantern in a usable loadout. The equipment
transaction selector sees current owned equipment and operational fuel, but
excludes the zero-fuel lantern before scoring and does not know that the
survival selector intends to refill it. Each therefore acts on a different
state: survival sees *refillable carried item + oil*, while equipment sees
*usable candidates only*. Each action makes the other condition true again.
The unconditional early return for restore at `policy.py:11464-11469` also
prevents the later `_light_refill_item` path (`:11491-11494`) from running.
This is the unhandled conflict described in (a), introduced when independent
light-selection owners were combined.

(d) Why the lantern remains empty despite oil; the `lit` observation
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For an identified equipped lantern, `_light_refill_item` at
`src/hengbot/policy_supply.py:153-156` returns carried oil whenever fuel is at
or below `LANTERN_REFILL_FUEL`. The capture's lantern is known with zero fuel
and the pack contains oil, so this predicate would supply a refill command if
reached. But while the torch is equipped, the earlier restore branch returns
at `policy.py:11464-11469`; after the restore's transaction equips the torch,
the next decision again takes that same branch. Thus refill is starved by
control-flow ordering, not by a lack of oil or by the known-lantern predicate.

For an *unknown* equipped lantern, `_light_refill_item` instead tests the
player grid's `lit` flag at `policy_supply.py:139-152`. The live protocol's
`GridState.lit` is always false, so that branch cannot observe an unknown
lantern still burning and may treat it as empty. This observation defect was
never corrected in this path; it does not explain this capture's known,
identified zero-turn lantern, but it is why that fallback is not a reliable
fuel signal.

(e) Torch-over-lantern report and regression classification
------------------------------------------------------------
Issue #46's reported torch choice has two possible observed forms. For the
capture loop, the optimizer selects the torch because the empty lantern is
excluded from candidates, while the restore selector puts it back before
refilling; this is the unhandled ownership conflict above. For an optimizer
comparison where both lights are usable, `light_source_quality` prefers a
lantern only on equal combat metrics; greater modeled offense/defense may still
select the torch. The 2026-10-03 report that an inferior light was equipped is
a case the existing score never fully handled: the quality score is a tie
breaker, rather than a consistent radius/remaining-turns decision. No specific
commit changed the quality score to prefer torch over lantern; the described
inferior-light case was never handled by the optimizer's score contract.

Attribution summary
-------------------
* `d6cd3975`: introduced restore-empty-lantern behavior; the interaction with
  the equipment transaction owner and the refill step was never handled.
* `56ac155d`: introduced the optimizer's permanent/lantern/torch tie-break;
  remaining-fuel/refillability and light preference ahead of combat ties were
  never handled.
* `fd660d96`: introduced unknown-lantern refill based on grid `lit`; the
  protocol's always-false `lit` observation was never handled here.
