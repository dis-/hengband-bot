# Round 2 (ISSUE #60)

## Why 177/177 HP died while the estimate was 118

Measured on the board (fixture row, decision 1995, turn 159544): race 1090 『ボルガ博士』 is awake, unhurt and
adjacent at (29,18); the player at (30,19) has AC 6, speed +0, and every other neighbour is a non-passable grid
(the mining pocket), so the monster's cell is the only passable neighbour. Inventory a..m has no teleport, phase or
recall scroll (one unknown scroll). The capture has no message after the melee key, so the cause of death is read from
the game source, not observed.

Hengband source (C:\hengband\src):
- The EXPLODE blow is the monster's own melee blow against the player. `monster-attack-describer.cpp:171-177` sets
  `explode`, and `monster-attack-player.cpp:240-244` then sets the blow's own damage to 0.
- SHATTER (`monster-attack-switcher.cpp:487-495`) calls `earthquake(monster position, radius 8)` when
  `damage > 23 || explode`, so an exploding SHATTER blow always starts it (underground, outside fixed quests:
  `earthquake.cpp:329-334`).
- `earthquake.cpp`: every grid except the centre collapses with 15% (`decide_collapse_positions`). If the player's grid
  collapses, the player dodges to an empty, uncollapsed neighbour (`decide_player_dodge_posistion`). If one exists,
  1 of 3 outcomes does no damage and 2 of 3 deal 10d4. If none exists, the player takes a fixed **200** direct hit
  (`process_player_damage_undodged`).
- After the blow, `monster_explode` kills the monster, and `monster-death.cpp:52-65 on_dead_explosion` projects a
  radius-3 ball of the blow dice (15d8, SHATTER -> ROCKET). At distance 1 the player gets (roll+1)/2, at most 60
  (`effect-player.cpp:208`). Killing it in melee triggers only this ball, at most 60 at distance 1.

On this board the only empty neighbour is the monster's own cell, so a collapse of the player's grid (15% of the
explosions that hit) is the 200 direct hit, and 200 >= 177. This is the only damage path in the source that can take
177 HP in one monster turn here. The bot's estimate was the 15d8 dice less AC, as ordinary melee: max 118, operational
81. It had no earthquake term, so the estimate was low by the 200 direct hit. Meleeing did not trigger the explosion.
The monster's own next blow did, and waiting adjacent gives that blow just the same.

## Change (replaces the round-1 change)

The round-1 `_decide` block and the no-wait filter are removed. They added a direct `_flee_step` call site, which
failed the item-sink lint and also the existing ladder-order test `test_the_decide_rungs_are_the_marked_calls_in_order`
(it asserts 81 decide rungs). The NOT CAUGHT no-wait hunk is gone with them. The town-work bookkeeping that round 1
regenerated is back at base, unchanged.

The estimate now uses the correct value, in the one shared place where all avoidance gates read it:
- `monster_melee_evaluator.add_earthquake_damage(distribution, dodge_cells)` adds one earthquake to a blow's
  distribution: P(direct hit 200) = 0.15 * 0.15^dodge_cells, P(10d4) = (0.15 - that) * 2/3. The game constants
  (15%, 200, 10d4, 2 of 3) are mirrored from `earthquake.cpp`, with a source comment. They are game facts, not tuning
  thresholds.
- `CombatMixin._melee_blow_distributions`: an EXPLODE+SHATTER blow, when `dungeon_level > 0`, gets the earthquake
  added to its reduced-dice distribution. Dodge cells are the player's passable neighbours without a monster.
- `threat_prediction`: the per-action maximum is the maximum of the same distributions. For every other blow this is
  identical to before, because the reduction is monotone.
- The existing dice term is kept for the explosion (pinned by the existing
  `test_exploding_monster_contributes_at_most_one_melee_attack`, EXPLODE/FIRE 8d8 = 64). It over-states the death ball
  (at most 60 at distance 1), so the corrected value is conservative.

No new rung, call site, policy attribute or threshold. The existing emergency rule (`predicted >= hp` -> stairs ->
escape scroll -> recall -> heal -> upstairs -> retreat step -> cornered attack -> wait) now handles the board.
