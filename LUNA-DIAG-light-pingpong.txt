Producer attribution and predicates
===================================

* `src/hengbot/policy.py:10701-10703` invokes `_darkness_recovery_key` before
  ordinary navigation, but both captured boards report `can_see_own_grid=True`.
  `_is_dark` at `src/hengbot/policy.py:13128-13134` therefore returns false;
  the darkness producer selected neither recorded action.
* Both recorded actions come from the ordinary block at
  `src/hengbot/policy.py:11638-11658`. `_light_to_wield` at
  `src/hengbot/policy.py:12993-13022` sees the equipped lantern at or below
  `LANTERN_REFILL_FUEL` and, because no refill item exists, allows a lower-ranked
  torch to replace it. On the next board it sees the fueled torch as rank 0 and
  the still-usable lantern as rank 1, so selects the lantern back.
* `_darkness_torch` at `src/hengbot/policy.py:13163-13184` has the separate
  directional torch-recovery behavior: it would select a carried torch for a
  low-fuel worn lantern in darkness, but it is not active on either recorded
  board. The measured cause here is the ordinary selector's asymmetric handling
  of an equipped low lantern versus a carried low lantern.
* `src/hengbot/policy_constants.py:390` sets `LANTERN_REFILL_FUEL = 1000`;
  line 392 sets `TORCH_REFILL_FUEL = 500`. These are existing refill bounds; no
  new bound is needed.
* `_light_refill_item` at `src/hengbot/policy_supply.py:131-164` only returns an
  oil flask found in the inventory for a known worn lantern at/below the lantern
  bound. Neither captured inventory contains oil. The lantern was therefore not
  refillable on these boards. The inventory listing contains only the opposite
  light, not any oil item.

Diagnosis
=========

The captured producer is `_light_to_wield` for both sides of the alternation;
`_darkness_recovery_key` does not run its darkness branch because the player
can see their own grid. At 984 fuel the low-lantern no-refill rule authorizes a
torch downgrade; after that swap, normal rank ordering upgrades to the same
low-fuel lantern again. I will make the no-refill low lantern ineligible as a
replacement candidate, while retaining the current equipped low-lantern escape
to a fueled torch. When oil is carried, the existing refill command will take
priority while the lantern is equipped.
