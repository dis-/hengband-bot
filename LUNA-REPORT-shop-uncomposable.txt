Diagnosis
---------
See LUNA-DIAG-shop-uncomposable.txt for source locations and both capture
boards. The visit did not leave and re-enter before either stop. After prior
in-store sale/inscription work, the continuation preflight intentionally
prioritized the first departure-blocking purchase (row h or f), while the
single stateful _shop call selected a different affordable quest purchase
(row n). The old emit gate compared the key only with the preflight row and
turned a valid selector disagreement into F2.

Chosen fix and bound
--------------------
Implemented option (b): keep _shop as the sole stateful operation producer.
When a continuation's buy letter differs from the pure preflight, resolve that
letter on the current store page and recheck the row identity, unique letter,
page one, verified screen, and affordability before committing the operation.
An absent/duplicate row, wrong page, unverified screen, invalid quantity, or
unaffordable emitted buy still falls through to the existing visible
shop-required-operation-uncomposable stop and keeps its diagnostic.

This does not add operations or visits. The existing STORE_STUCK_LIMIT of 8
still bounds the ledger. The pins start at 2 operations for Alchemist and 6 for
General; after the ordinary purchase and required blocker they are at 4 and 8,
respectively, before the same-visit leave.
