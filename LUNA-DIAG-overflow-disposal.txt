## Diagnosis

Two independent gates cause the stop: no identified-cursed-item priority means t remains blocked, and `_has_town_economic_path` mistakes hypothetical equipment/shop eligibility for an action that a producer will really execute on this visit. The Home path also declines because the equipment optimizer has no calibrated target loadout. Productive producers run earlier in the town pipeline and did decline; the final fallback must honor that actual result while retaining procurement, identification, survival, and owner reservations.
