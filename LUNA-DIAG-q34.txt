2. Cloaker death / fixed target visibility stop

Exact predicate: in src/hengbot/policy_quest.py:3057-3060,
`fixed_target_cell_visible` first requires a known, currently observed cell.
It accepts `cell.in_view`, but then explicitly returns false when

Observed facts: the floor is again `(0,5,34)`. The cloaker (race 243) is at
