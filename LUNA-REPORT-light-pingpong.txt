Change
======

In `src/hengbot/policy.py`, `_light_to_wield` now refuses to select a known,
low-fuel carried lantern when no oil is carried. The existing low-fuel worn
lantern rule still selects a fueled torch, so a lantern can yield when it is
nearly exhausted; once the torch is worn, the same low-fuel lantern cannot
replace it. When oil is carried and the low-fuel lantern is equipped, the
existing refill path wins before any light swap. No constant, threshold,
reason, or new helper was added. The directional darkness recovery remains
compatible: it also chooses a fueled torch for a low-fuel worn lantern and
leaves an already working torch in place.

The manifest was regenerated after the final source edit. It reports 826
candidates and 20,989 call sites, up 3 call sites from the base manifest; the
candidate census is unchanged. No new producer/helper baseline entries or
stuck-family reason were needed. No existing test assertions were edited.
