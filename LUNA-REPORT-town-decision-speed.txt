Town decision speed change report

Branch: town-decision-speed-1017
Base: 9ec39c05bd289930019725dfc6e8a23a65269430

Diagnosis

The baseline profiler confirmed that town need evaluation repeatedly rebuilt the
same fixed-quest join and repeated retention calculations. Across capture A,
_town_need_candidates ran 2,736 times, _retention_reservation_baseline_detail
ran 405,226 times, _uncached_fixed_quest_head ran 406,258 times, and
_known_fixed_quests ran 1,227,052 times. Across capture B, the corresponding
counts were 1,828, 534,418, 534,516, and 536,442.

The dependency audit and cache boundaries are recorded in
LUNA-DIAG-town-decision-speed.txt. Retention reservation and surplus values
are reused only during one read-only town-need query. Fixed quest joins and
heads are memoized only for the current choose_key decision, keyed by the exact
quest values, snapshot identity, relevant town/player fields, offer set, and
static quest table identities. The cache storage uses ContextVars with
try/finally resets; no cache attribute is added to policy state or checkpoints.

Changes

* Memoized retention reservation and surplus computations inside one
  _town_need_candidates query.
* Memoized the known fixed-quest join and fixed-quest head within one decision.
* Added 134 recorded decision-board rows as raw UTF-8 gzip fixtures: 85 boards
  for A and 49 for B. The source captures have no decision_sequence field;
  extraction retained their store/player_turn board rows and omitted setup
  rows.
* Added a cache-disabled replay oracle that compares (key, last_reason) and
  restorable policy state after every decision. It removes only elapsed-time
  fields (ms, shelf_observe_ms, dry_run_ms) from in-store telemetry before the
  state comparison.
* Added recorded-board call-count pins for retention computations and fixed
  quest computations. No existing test assertions were changed.
* Updated the generated town work inventory and helper review entries. The
  census changed from 826 to 831 candidates, from 20,957 to 21,000 call sites,
  with 112 registry sites and 102 ladder sites unchanged. The structural test
  module passed without edits.

Call-count pins

On a recorded high-inventory A board, uncached/cached counts were 3,075/656 for
retention baseline computations and 2,952/656 for surplus computations. On a
recorded B board, fixed quest join computations were 86/2 and fixed quest head
computations were 84/2. The test asserts the cached counts are less than half
for retention and lower for both fixed-quest computations.
