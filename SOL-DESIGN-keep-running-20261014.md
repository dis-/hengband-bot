PROPOSED — Resolution is a sequence across decisions, not a second producer selection inside a decision:

1. Validate location, fresh acknowledged command-loop board, physical input/gear debt and survival. Unknown prompt/protocol or irreconcilable debt -> H. A posted operation cannot be closed as “never happened”. Known safe UI release uses its existing owner, then awaits its observed receipt.
2. Normalize exhausted work identity; append its closure once, persist it, release unposted intents and reservations through their existing owner. Recurrent pairs close **all business participants** together. Failed required procurement can close as work while its underlying safety deficit remains HARD.
3. Mark re-plan for the **next** decision. Admission and continuation both reject closed work. Normal surviving producers may finish necessary work within remaining budget. If no actionable work remains, the next decision selects existing departure/fallback under the minimum. No filler WAIT/wander is a resolution.
4. If the minimum holds, arm a departure commitment: no new optional work, no return to the failed supplier. Existing departure/fundraising owner controls route and command. Revalidate HARD facts on every entry/send boundary. A real new threat is handled by survival; it cannot reopen optional work.
5. If minimum fails, only already-existing unclosed suppliers or bounded safe income fallback may act. Exhaustion of these paths -> H with exact deficit/route/protocol cause. An exhausted departure route closes that route and tries the finite alternatives; all exhausted -> H.

PROPOSED — Work identity is `(character_run_id, visit_epoch, town_id, business_kind, target_bucket)`; canonical producer work/goal identity supplies the target, not decision sequence, turn, random claim ID, slot letter, changing gold or full progress vector. PROPOSED — Proof of no reopening: for a visit, closed set `C` is monotone (`C' = C union {w}`); admission, plan rebuild and continuation all require `w not in C`.
