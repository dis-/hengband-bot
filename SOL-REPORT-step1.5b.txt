Implementation

F1: policy_home.py, policy_shop.py, policy_town.py, policy_equipment.py,
policy.py. Read the open Home page before releasing a staged deposit tail as
well as before composing an open-page deposit. A 240/240 page starts relief;
no overweight deposit is posted into it. Mandatory overweight batches are
never put into the optional deferred retry. Equipment-origin Home blocks
carry visit-local provenance; every direct Home blocked-store eligibility
read goes through _town_store_blocked_under_applicable_bound(need=...).
Weight work ignores only the equipment-origin block, preserving independent
Home pass/approach bounds and step1.5a's equipment tombstone. Observed Home
success still releases a numeric pass block regardless of its old limit.

F2: carried Home-relief disposal is considered only when the pack is full.
Its selection and emission probe the existing departure ledger. Removing a
stack cannot worsen a required departure shortage or reduce Identify charges
below min(20, the previously carried charges). Home shelf relief remains
available for a non-full pack; a non-full pack gets no carried fallback destroy.

F3: separate _overweight_surplus_sale_key, admitted only after full Home relief
has no legal candidate while overweight. _retention_surplus defines every
quantity; required counts and departure gates are unchanged. Candidates are
sorted by lost sale value, and each selected stack's entire surplus is sold
through _store_sell_key/_batch_sale_entry and the existing in-store commit.
Regular shops, Bookstore and Black Market are tested for acceptance; only
items with no remaining buyer use the checked partial-destroy sink. Protected
equipment, artifacts, bounty items, reserved items, and pending/planned mining
supplies remain protected. Insufficient legal shedding stops immediately with
town:blocked:overweight-home-full-no-legal-relief.

An observed disposal appends time, item, count, actual gold delta, Home stock,
weight before/after and visit epoch to overweight-surplus-disposal.jsonl.
Decision reasons are town:overweight-surplus-sold:home-full and
town:overweight-surplus-destroyed:home-full. The durable ledger records the
posted operation before its effect and records completed signatures. A second
disposal of the same normalized identity in that visit stops visibly with
town:blocked:overweight-surplus-rebuy-loop. Identity strips regenerated display
inscriptions and excludes count, position and turn. A fresh process reloads
the active epoch and ledger; leaving town ends that epoch.
