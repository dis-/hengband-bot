PLAN-STEP: 3 - ISSUE #1 diagnosis

Observed failure
----------------
In both captures the bot stayed inside one store visit, completed its in-store
inscribe/sell work, and then stopped when the continuation preflight selected a
departure-blocking buy but the stateful shop selector emitted a different,
also-needed buy.

Selection path (b8f5ac67)
------------------------
`src/hengbot/policy_instore.py:193-265` is `_in_store_selection`. Once the
entry ledger has `ops > 0` (`:217-230`), it calls
`_departure_blocking_page_purchase(snapshot)` and returns that buy before
considering further ordinary sales or purchases. The returned purchase is
chosen in `src/hengbot/policy_shop.py:3078-3146`, by
`_departure_blocking_page_purchase_pure`: it filters live purchase rungs to
town needs that still block departure, then chooses the first affordable,
page-available candidate in those rungs. This chose Alchemist row `h` and
General row `f` in the two captures.

The actual command comes from `_in_store_emit` in
`src/hengbot/policy_instore.py:522-606`, which calls `_shop(snapshot)` once.
`src/hengbot/policy_shop.py:5072-5081` runs `_next_purchase(snapshot)` after
the shop's higher-priority sale checks. The ordinary selector delegates through
`_next_purchase_unreserved` (`policy_shop.py:2996-3010`) to
`_legacy_next_purchase_unreserved` (`policy_shop.py:3148+`) and uses its
ordinary procurement ordering. That path chose row `n`: it is the earlier
quest-procurement purchase in the ordinary ordering. Thus the continuation's
departure-blocker-first ordering and `_shop`'s quest-purchase-first ordering
can validly select different affordable items on the same page. `_in_store_emit`
refused to post the different row under the preflight identity and returned
None; the continuation then treated that disagreement as uncomposable and
raised the visible F2 stop.

First-operation scope
---------------------
The captured ordering conflict is a continuation-only case. The special
departure-blocking selection runs only after `ledger.ops > 0`
(`policy_instore.py:217-230`). On the first operation, `_in_store_selection`
falls through to `_next_purchase`, the ordinary purchase selector used later by
`_shop`; it does not prioritize the departure blocker over the quest purchase.
A first-page selection/emission mismatch from another stateful producer remains
possible, but `_in_store_emit(first=True)` hands that `_shop` key to the
existing outside composition path rather than raising this continuation stop
(`policy_instore.py:590-600`).
