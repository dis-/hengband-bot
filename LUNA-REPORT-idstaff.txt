# Identify staff sale selector fix

Issue: https://github.com/dis-/hengband-bot/issues/35

Changed `ShopMixin._find_device_sale` in `src/hengbot/policy_shop.py`. Identify staffs can now reach sale only through the dedicated surplus/release selector, and the selector refuses an Identify staff sale when the remaining carried charges would fall below 20. The release selector continues to choose the fewest-charge staff first.

Regression pin: the recorded 2026-10-06 09:16 carrying state (20-charge newly bought staff plus two staffs with 11 charges combined) stays unsold even when the generic device classifier calls Identify staffs non-useful. Control: with five Identify staffs totaling 36 charges, the fifth staff with the fewest charges is selected and 31 charges remain.
