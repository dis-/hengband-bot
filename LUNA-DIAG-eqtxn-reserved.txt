Scope and history

The captures are from baseline 35fcd16f. HEAD 218c95e9 only adds the task file, so
neither refusal path was introduced by a code change on this branch. This scene had
not been handled at the baseline.

A - combat weapon restoration and the invalid deposit address

- At capture A decision 750 the emitted command is `di`: Home deposit (`d`) plus
  address `i`. The immediately preceding transaction decision is travel-home. The
  captured game response says the pack has letters a-h only. The Home board's
  protocol inventory also assigns `i` and `j` (turn 126058; the `i` row is tval 35,
  sval 1, while `j` is the digging tool), exposing the address disagreement: the
  serializer trusts an observed item.slot even though the game's accepted pack
  address range for this command is only a-h. In `policy_equipment.py:2227-2252`,
  the deposit predicate searches by identity/move identity, then
  `policy_equipment.py:2327-2335` passes the found item's current slot to
  `reserved_item_command`; no predicate verifies that the resulting pack address
  exists in the game's current selectable range. Once the later Home board no
  longer contains the planned target, the exact `target is None` predicate at
  `policy_equipment.py:2241-2252` records `deposit-missing` and exits Home.
- The following captured decision is `home:identify-staff-reserve-unavailable` with
  ESC and `store_type=7`: the identification owner itself leaves Home. On returning
  to town, the equipment session still requires Home. At
  `policy_equipment.py:2583-2600`, failure to get a Home step (including the
  entrance arbitration state after leaving Home) adds `home-route-unavailable` and
  returns WAIT. Thus "unavailable" describes the current approach-step result, not
  the permanent absence of a map route.
- `policy_equipment.py:2543-2549` next sees the non-executable session, calls
  `_abandon_blocked_equipment_transaction`, and records `abandon-blocked`.
  `policy_town.py:1792-1850` steps off the entrance while preserving that owner.
  The abandonment is attempt-local: `policy_equipment.py:1691-1830` can reconstruct
  restoration work, and clearing the session invalidates optimization for a fresh
  search. There was no marker saying this visit's planned Home deposit had already
  been disproved. Consequently the same plan is eligible again. The captured
  decisions alternate route-unavailable and abandon-blocked until the repeated
  route signature becomes terminal at `policy_equipment.py:1647-1668` and
  `:1818-1822`.

B - the reserved torch deposit

- The 18:06:35 Home transaction follows purchases of three torches. The captured
  plan's remaining Home-prepare actions are torch deposits (identities
  5e6e3382b7319e8c and f53f3657deb1e21d twice). The carried {@v0=g} torches appear
  in `retention_reservations` under `carry-strategy:lit_torch`. At the first
  refused deposit, `deposit_keep_conflict` says the session identity is
  f53f3657deb1e21d, `in_keep_set=true`, and the reserving branch is
  `carry-strategy:lit_torch`. The plan was computed for the post-purchase board
  before this live reservation was applied, so its transaction contains a transfer
  the current board says must remain in the pack.
- `policy_equipment.py:2291-2307` correctly finds the target and tests its current
  reservation. It abandons the stale transaction and exits Home as
  `retain-reserved`. However, like A, this only ends the current attempt. The
  unchanged reservation remains true, and clearing the optimization signature
  permits the optimizer to create the same deposit plan again.
- A's missing-target refusal and B's reserved-target refusal therefore need a
  visit-local completion record. A generic route failure alone must not close the
  visit: the existing repeated-route final stop remains available for a genuinely
  unbounded route failure.

Required behavior: after a Home deposit is observed missing or retained, abandon
that optional equipment transaction once, retain any separately reconstructed
restoration work, and suppress its replanning until the town visit epoch changes.
The decision reason and closure record provide bounded evidence; other town work
and departure remain eligible.
