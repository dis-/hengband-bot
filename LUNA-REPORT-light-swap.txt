PLAN-STEP: 3 / ISSUE: #46 — Light swap report

Diagnosis
=========
See `LUNA-DIAG-light-swap.txt` for the capture facts, base-revision predicate
locations, and commit attribution. In short, the survival path repeatedly
wielded a carried zero-turn lantern because oil was present; the equipment
optimizer then excluded that lantern as non-operational and chose the fueled
torch. The restore branch returned before the refill branch could oil the
lantern. The live capture records 43 restore decisions and 42 torch equip
decisions, alternating with player light radius 2/1. The capture boards show
five oil flasks, an empty known lantern, and a torch with 1,866 turns remaining.

`_light_refill_item` correctly offers oil for a known equipped lantern at zero
fuel. Its unknown-lantern fallback relies on `GridState.lit`, which is always
false in the live protocol, so that branch cannot distinguish a burning
unknown lantern. This did not explain the captured board, whose lantern was
known and empty; the refill action was starved by the earlier restore return.
The optimizer's pre-existing score prefers lantern over torch (quality 1 vs
0) only when combat metrics are equal; it excludes the known empty lantern
before evaluating loadouts. The missing case was refillability and the
conflicting owners, not a score that ranks a torch above a lantern in an
otherwise equal operational comparison.

Change
======
`_empty_lantern_to_restore` now returns no lantern while the equipped light is
usable or unidentified. This preserves the current working light and leaves
light selection to the equipment optimizer while it has a usable candidate.
Once that light is exhausted, the existing recovery sequence can equip the
empty lantern and `_light_refill_item` can refill it. The existing light rank
and optimizer tie-break both order permanent light > lantern > torch. The
optimizer then keeps the refilled lantern on equal combat scores. An exhausted
light allows at most one empty-lantern restoration followed by one refill;
with a usable worn light, the restoration count is zero, so the captured
alternation cannot recur. The existing Quest 34 throwing-torch reservation
code was not changed.

One existing assertion was updated: `test_restores_empty_pack_lantern_after_oil_is_acquired`
expected the bot to replace a fueled torch with an empty lantern. That test's
torch had 640 turns remaining, so the expectation directly contradicted the
requested rule. Its replacement asserts that the torch is kept and retains the
separate check that an equipped empty lantern is refilled from carried oil.
