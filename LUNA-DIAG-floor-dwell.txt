Why the bot never leaves (code at 08257674 + measured row fields)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Descent is closed at the depth cap: required_depth_gates(31) = {resist_chaos}
   (policy_constants.DEPTH_ABILITY_REQUIREMENTS 31-39); depth_safety.has in every row lacks
   resist_chaos. So 30F is the deepest permitted floor; a known down stair (00:47, 01:29, 07:11)
   is never a descent target. (08:04 is 29F: no down stair was ever seen in the window.)
2. The floor is left only by (a) a town-return trigger or (b) rule 9 in policy.py ~12376
   "Nothing to explore: take any known stairs" which runs only after exploration, probing,
   secret-wall search all return nothing. With light radius 1 on a 198x44 floor the
   frontier set GROWS during the 30 minutes (e.g. 126 -> 867, 88 -> 1086), so (b) is never reached.
3. The return trigger 'pack-full' (policy_town._should_start_town_return: len(inventory) >= 23)
   is pre-empted by the in-dungeon destroy rungs: the pack sits at 22/23 (free 1) for most of every
   full window; each new pickup fills it (free 0 for 6-17 rows) and inventory:destroy-worthless-junk
   / destroy-disposable-item / trigger-autodestroy frees one slot again (e.g. 21:29 window:
   44 pickups, 10 + 4 destroys, 12 autodestroys; picked-then-destroyed items: 洞窟オークの骨,
   洞窟オークの死体, グレイ・ベトベトの死体, cursed 硬革ブーツ, トラップ感知の巻物, 回復モンスターの魔法棒).
   Destroying remains before a return is the owner decision of 10-18 (#70); here it also
   keeps the return from ever starting.
4. Within the window much walking is repeated room sweeps: cells walked 2-3 times on average
   (visit histogram 07:11 window: 625x1, 199x2, 229x3; 00:22 window: 186x1, 351x2, 66x3, ... up to 12),
   e.g. 06:40-06:49 the same room rows 6-30 / cols 5-23 was swept three times in boustrophedon.
   INFERRED (not measured, no policy internals logged): torch-lit floor in dark rooms is 'known'
   but not 'marked', and the frontier predicate (_is_remembered_frontier, policy.py ~20066) tests
   neighbours against _marked_t, so a swept dark-room cell stays a frontier until
   FRONTIER_EXHAUST_VISITS (8) visits or until probed. map_memory marked is only 20-50 % of known
   in every capture (e.g. 1565 known / 711 marked, 2907 / 570), consistent with this.
