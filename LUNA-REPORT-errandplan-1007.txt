The regression came from treating ordinary `LEAVE_STORE_KEY` results as terminal shop refusals: a stale or empty `last_reason` let ordinary shelves with nothing wanted enter the new moved-cursor settlement path, which returned no command and could release an unrelated Home visit instead of advancing the current errand stop. The fix keeps ordinary no-operation settlement on the current stop and allows moved-cursor settlement and visit closure only for the recorded sell/rebuy refusal or named procurement-home terminals, preserving the crosstown refusal handoff.
