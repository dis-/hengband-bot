Policy interpretation checked against source

The operator's broad dead-state model is plausible, but two parts are not measured facts:
(1) "identification-flow" is not an ownership verdict; and (2) the archived rows do not
prove that reserved_item_command returned None for the original process. Producer reach,
selected catalog objects, and private reservation state are missing from these captures.
The defect that can be fixed and pinned is the predicate mismatch: actionability currently
accepts selected pack items without the producer's reservation check, and accepts Home items
without all of the producer's arming conditions. The change will make both decisions use the
same producer-target predicate. If that yields no target, the existing optional-equipment
current-loadout departure route remains subject to required-depth abilities.
