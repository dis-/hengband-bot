# Step 1.5a + 1.5b merge-gate regressions

## 1. Free-pack carried junk

- Cause: full-Home relief only considered carried disposal when the pack was
  full. With a free pack, the selector skipped an unneeded carried bottle and
  advanced to the overweight-surplus route. The attempted fallback also had
  to preserve the existing full-pack behavior and keep required supplies and
  equipment under their existing owners.
- Class: policy ordering regression (F2 before F3).
- Change: after eligible Home shelf discard/identification candidates are
  exhausted, a non-full pack may use safe carried junk as a pack-only relief
  target. Equipment and Identify staves remain excluded; retention,
  equipment-reservation, and departure-preservation checks still gate the
  candidate. Full-pack selection is unchanged.
- Evidence: the existing
  `test_free_pack_carried_junk_is_not_falsely_reported_protected` passes
  unchanged. Step 1.5b P4 and P7 also pass in the final validation.

## 2. Recorded no-legal-relief deposit

- Cause: the recorded board is overweight: carried weight 1932, limit 1800.
  The deposit is therefore departure blocking, so 1.5b F1b applies and the
  optional no-relief deferral is not legal. This is the intended 2026-10-10
  decision, not a policy regression.
- Class: obsolete test expectation for intended F1b behavior.
- Change: declared the constructed boundary as 1.5b F1b / the 2026-10-10
  decision and changed the test to assert key `9` and reason
  `town:blocked:overweight-home-full-no-legal-relief` exactly.
- Evidence: the fixture weight and limit are asserted in the test. The updated
  test passes in the final validation.

## 3. Supply-ledger observer memo

- Cause: `_compute_supply_ledger` reads `_town_store_attempted` through
  `_supply_page_offers`, but the observer memo key omitted it. A capture that
  changed attempted-store state could reuse stale ledger results.
- Class: cache invalidation defect.
- Change: added an immutable `frozenset(self._town_store_attempted)` to
  `_supply_ledger_observer_inputs`.
- Evidence: the static observer-input scan and the complete
  `tests.test_supply_ledger_observer_memo` module pass in the final validation.
